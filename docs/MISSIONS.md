# Mission catalog

All missions are sequential. The yellow objective ring is the destination. Rewards affect only local prototype cash.

| # | Mission | Goal | Type | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Welcome to Early City | Downtown at (1860,1110) | On foot or car | $250 |
| 2 | First Wheels | East garage at (2920,1840) | In vehicle | $650 |
| 3 | Courier Shift | Pier at (680,2450) | In vehicle | $900 |
| 4 | Night Run | North at (3220,75) in 95 seconds | Timed | $1,400 |
| 5 | City Explorer | Lookout at (84,2200) | On foot or car | $1,800 |
| 6 | Southbound | South at (1780,2720) | On foot or car | $500 |
| 7 | Garage Appointment | East garage at (2920,1840) | In vehicle | $850 |
| 8 | The Long Way Home | Starter garage at (235,155) | In vehicle | $1,200 |
| 9 | Midnight Errand | Harbor at (1160,2550) in 110 seconds | Timed | $1,600 |

A timed mission resets its timer after expiration, rather than restarting the whole game. An N-key debug skip pays nothing. No dynamic mission scripting/editor is included yet.
