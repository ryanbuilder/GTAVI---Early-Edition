# World bible

## Creative direction
A fictional sunny coastal city with an understated crime-fiction tone. The current project uses original top-down schematic art and does not reproduce a recognizable commercial game map.

## Regions and landmarks
**North grid:** residential blocks and major east-west roadways, useful for sprint and timed objectives. **Downtown:** dense mixed-use blocks and center checkpoint at (1860,1110). **Eastside:** auto garage at (2920,1840), with a driving objective. **West district:** lookout target at (84,2200). **South/harbor:** courier and timed destinations around (680,2450) and (1160,2550). These labels describe intended fiction, not separately rendered architectural sets.

## Environmental story plan (not implemented)
Design unique storefront signage, neon harbor lights, public transit, rooftop routes, nighttime crowds, safehouses, gas stations, and recognizable landmarks. Add an original music and radio plan only after rights and licensing review.

## Art direction rules
Use generic original props, fictional vehicle badges and invented business names. Keep the prototype clear at small resolutions. Prioritize navigation/readability over ornamental detail. External inspiration is not a license to redistribute ripped models, textures, audio or proprietary leaked files.
