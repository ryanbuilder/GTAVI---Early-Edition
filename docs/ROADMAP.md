# Development roadmap

## v0.1 · Proof of concept (complete)

- Basic map and player movement
- Starter drivable car and checkpoints
- Minimal HUD and minimap

## v0.2 · Living City (implemented)

- Expanded city with distinct building and park cells
- Moving traffic, wandering pedestrians, dynamic time tint
- Five missions, basic money, speed, car health and wanted rating
- Full map, pause/help screens, manual save/load, periodic autosave
- Improved interface and design documentation

## v0.3 · Simulation quality (planned)

- Intersection control and traffic lights
- Vehicle-to-vehicle physical responses and pedestrian safety logic
- Police pursuit agents and functional evasion
- Audio and graphics settings
- Separate state/simulation/render modules and automated tests

## v0.4 · Mission systems (planned)

- Mission scripting DSL with conditions, branching and failures
- Mission selection, NPC interactions, dialogue and quest journal
- Garage / repair gameplay and spending economy
- Save schema migrations

## v0.5 · 3D vertical slice (planned)

- Select 3D engine and build a fully original neighborhood
- Third-person camera, character controller, 3D vehicle handling
- Terrain and lighting, accessible graphics presets
- Gameplay capture recorded from an actual running build

## Future exploration (not committed)

AI-driven NPC schedules, apartment interiors, controller support, multiplayer prototypes, weather, modding APIs and packaging into a desktop installer. No public release dates promised.
