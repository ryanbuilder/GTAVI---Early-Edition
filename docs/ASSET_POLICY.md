# Asset provenance and publishing policy

1. Only use original work, properly licensed third-party assets or materials for which contributors have explicit permission.
2. Do not commit leaked source code, unpublished artwork, recovered credentials or reverse-engineered confidential content.
3. Do not market this project as an official Rockstar product or as evidence of possession of a playable unreleased GTA game.
4. Label synthetic video **AI-generated concept** and distinguish it from screen capture of actual code.
5. Record creator, origin, license, permission and attribution for every future non-original asset in a dedicated asset manifest before merging.
6. Remove any disputed content while ownership and permissions are reviewed.
